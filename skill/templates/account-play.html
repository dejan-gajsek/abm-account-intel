How to find a bridge (about 10 minutes)

  1. Open each committee member in Sales Navigator. Check TeamLink and the rep's own mutual connections.
  2. Rank bridges: customer, partner, company leader, former colleague, shared school or association.
  3. Send the intro request. Wait 5 business days before the cold email to that person.
  4. Log the bridge and answer as a CRM note.

Guardrails

  • CASL and CAN-SPAM on every email: sender name, mailing address, working unsubscribe. Opt-outs same day.
  • Calls dialed by hand. No calls to mobiles flagged Do Not Call.
  • One-to-one only. These contacts stay non-marketing unless the user says otherwise.
