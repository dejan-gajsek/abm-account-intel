Our angle

Make wholesale reps faster inside HubSpot, without ripping out Shopify

Campflannel already sells online. The harder work is the human sales motion: briefed calls, clean follow-ups, and a CRM that stays usable as the wholesale book grows. That is where Glenbrief fits.

1. Account briefs before every call

A one-page brief pulled from HubSpot notes, past orders, and public shop signals. For: Avery Quinn, Riley Chen, Lex Park.

2. Follow-ups that start from the CRM

Draft notes and next steps from deal stage, last meeting, and open tasks, so reps send the same day. For: Avery Quinn, Samira Okonkwo, Theo Vargas.

3. Hygiene that does not need a weekly cleanup

Auto-suggested properties and staged next actions after calls, so RevOps stops chasing empty fields. For: Riley Chen, Casey Brooks.

Warm introductions

Find a bridge before the cold email

A second-degree LinkedIn connection who knows someone on the committee can turn a cold email into an introduction. The rep runs this step in Sales Navigator. Enrichment tools and HubSpot do not hold mutual connections.

How to find a bridge (about 10 minutes)

  1. Open each committee member in Sales Navigator. Check TeamLink and the rep's own mutual connections.
  2. Rank bridges: a happy customer first, then a partner, then a company leader, then a former colleague, then a shared school or association.
  3. Send the intro request below to the best one. Wait 5 business days before the cold email to that person.
  4. Log it in HubSpot as a note on the contact: who bridged, and the answer.

Leads to check first (example ranking)

  • Current Glenbrief customers in outdoor or DTC. Ask whether they know Avery or Riley.
  • Shopify Plus partner contacts. Theo Vargas likely sits in that network. Useful only if the rep already knows someone there.
  • Former employers. If Avery came from another brand with a HubSpot shop, check mutuals from that company.
  • Alex's own Portland network. Check mutuals with Avery Quinn first.

These bridge leads are part of the fictional example. A real play would name only people and firms the rep is cleared to involve.

Alex Rivera

Offer ladder

Low ask first, scoped pilot last

  1. Opinion ask: "Where do your wholesale reps lose the most time before and after a HubSpot call?"
  2. Share-back: a short summary of patterns from similar DTC + wholesale teams, sent only to people who answered.
  3. Workshop: 60 minutes with Sales, RevOps, and Partnerships. They leave with three HubSpot workflows ranked for Campflannel.
  4. Scoped pilot: one workflow with the success measure agreed before anything is built.

Objections

What we'll hear, and what to say

"We already have HubSpot"

"Good. We sit on top of it. The point is less time in empty fields and more time on the accounts that matter."

"We're already doing AI"

"Useful. Where does it show up for wholesale reps today? I'm asking because most teams have AI in the storefront long before the CRM motion is tidy."

"Not a priority right now"

"Understood. When do you plan the next wholesale cycle? I can check back then, and I can send a one-page summary of what other HubSpot wholesale teams are seeing if that helps."

"Just send me something"

"Happy to. So it's worth reading: is pre-call prep or same-day follow-up the bigger drag? I'll send the note that fits."

"What about our data and security?"

"Your HubSpot data stays in HubSpot. We'd walk Dana and Riley through scopes and access before anything starts."

"What does it cost?"

"It depends on the workflow, so we agree the success measure first. The workshop is where we size it, and it's useful even if we don't go further."

Measures

What good looks like for one account

Working targets for the play, not forecasts.

30 days

5 or more of the committee connected on LinkedIn or replied. At least one bridge tested.

60 days

A first conversation with Quinn, Chen, or Okonkwo. A workshop booked.

90 days

The workshop held with at least three leaders. A qualified opportunity with an agreed success measure.

Guardrails

  • CASL and CAN-SPAM on every email: sender name, mailing address, working unsubscribe. Opt-outs handled the same day.
  • Calls dialed by hand. No calls to mobiles flagged Do Not Call (Brooks, Ruiz).
  • No marketing emails. These are non-marketing contacts, so one-to-one only.
